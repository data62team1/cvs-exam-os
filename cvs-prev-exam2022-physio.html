Q1 Written

Compare and contrast: 1. Heterometric autoregulation vs Homeometric autoregulation 2. Isometric contraction phase of cardiac cycle vs Isometric relaxation phase of cardiac cycle

1. Heterometric vs. Homeometric Autoregulation:
Heterometric Autoregulation:
1)Definition: intrinsic regulation of contraction force dependent on initial
muscle fiber length (preload / EDV)
2)Trigger: increased end-diastolic volume (EDV) or venous return
3)Mechanism: increased initial fiber length → increased contractile system
sensitivity to Ca²⁺ → increased active cross-bridge formation
4)Governing Law: Starling's law of the heart ("force of contraction ∝ initial
fiber length / EDV")
5)Physiological Role: beat-to-beat balancing of stroke volume between right and
left ventricles

Homeometric Autoregulation:
1)Definition: intrinsic regulation of contraction force independent of initial
muscle fiber length
2)Trigger: sustained increase in afterload (arterial blood pressure)
3)Mechanism: increased coronary perfusion / internal myocardial stretch (Anrep
effect) → increased contractility at normal EDV
4)Governing Law: operates independently of Starling's initial force-length
relationship
5)Physiological Role: secondary adaptation to sustained arterial pressure
elevation (always preceded by a short heterometric phase)

2. Isometric Contraction vs. Isometric Relaxation Phase:
Isometric Contraction Phase:
1)Definition: ventricular contraction without change in ventricular volume
2)Duration: 0.05 seconds
3)Valve Status: all 4 valves closed (AV valves close at onset → produces 1st
heart sound, S1)
4)Pressure Change: sharp rise in intraventricular pressure (0 to 80 mmHg in
left ventricle)
5)Volume State: constant at end-diastolic volume (EDV = 130 mL)

Isometric Relaxation Phase:
1)Definition: ventricular relaxation without change in ventricular volume
2)Duration: 0.06 seconds
3)Valve Status: all 4 valves closed (semilunar valves close at onset → produces
2nd heart sound, S2)
4)Pressure Change: sharp drop in intraventricular pressure (110 to 0 mmHg in
left ventricle)
5)Volume State: constant at end-systolic volume (ESV = 55 mL)

التنظيم الذاتي والمراحل الإيزومترية بالقلب

Heterometric autoregulation يعتمد على ==تغيير طول الألياف العضلية الابتدائي== (Preload / EDV) لضبط الـ Stroke volume طبقاً لـ ||قانون ستارلينج||. بينما Homeometric autoregulation بيزود قوة الانقباض عند ثبات طول الألياف محافِظاً على الـ EDV عند ارتفاع الـ Afterload المتواصل كـ ||Anrep effect||.

أما في الدورة القلبية: في Isometric contraction phase، تنقبض العضلة وجميع الصمامات مقفولة، فيرتفع الضغط بسرعة من

0 إلى 80 مم زئبق
عند حجم ثابت EDV = 130 mL وببدايتها يُسمع الصوت القلبي الأول S1. في Isometric relaxation phase، تنبسط العضلة وجميع الصمامات مقفولة، فينخفض الضغط بسرعة من
110 إلى 0 مم زئبق
عند حجم ثابت ESV = 55 mL وببدايتها يُسمع الصوت القلبي الثاني S2.
